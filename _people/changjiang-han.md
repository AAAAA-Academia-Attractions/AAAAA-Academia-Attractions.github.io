Changjiang Han is a second-year M.Sc. student in Machine Learning at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI), supervised by Prof. Xue (Steve) Liu in the Cyber-Physical Intelligence Lab. He received his B.Eng. from Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST), where he received the Outstanding Thesis Award and Outstanding Graduate Award.

His research focuses on agentic AI, with particular interests in agent memory architectures and generative recommendation systems. His prior experience includes a research internship at JD.com and work on brain-computer interfaces and neuromorphic computing at Zhejiang University’s State Key Laboratory of Brain-Machine Intelligence.
