Jikun Kang is an AI researcher and Lead Member of Technical Staff at Salesforce, where he works on next-generation agent architectures. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from McGill University and Mila. His work spans deep reinforcement learning, agent evaluation harnesses, and interactive intelligence, focusing on developing scalable agent memory, computer-using agents (CUAs), and robust decision-making frameworks.
