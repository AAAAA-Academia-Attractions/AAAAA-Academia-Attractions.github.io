Tianyu Wei is a Ph.D. student in Mathematical Research at Imperial College London. He received his M.Eng. with First Class Honours in Biomedical Engineering from Imperial College London in 2024. His research investigates higher-order dependencies in compositional multi-task learning and machine learning for computational neuroscience, with applications in brain EEG dynamics during sleep and clinical neuro-technologies.
