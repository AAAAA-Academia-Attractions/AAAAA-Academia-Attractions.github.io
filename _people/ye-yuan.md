Ye Yuan is a last-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at McGill University and Mila, supervised by Prof. Xue (Steve) Liu. He received his B.Sc. in Honours Computer Science from McGill in 2022.

His research develops vision-language models, large language models, and score-based generative models for intelligent systems, with applications in offline black-box optimization, knowledge-centric NLP, and agentic systems. Full biography, news, and the complete publication list live on his [personal homepage](https://stevenyuan666.github.io/).
