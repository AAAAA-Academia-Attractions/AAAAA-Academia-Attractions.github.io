Frank Kang is a financial engineer based in Toronto, Canada. He received his M.Sc. in Computer Science from Imperial College London.

His work centers on large language models, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and multi-agent systems, with a focus on robust software infrastructure and evaluation harnesses for agentic workflows. His prior research and engineering experience spans reinforcement learning for quantitative finance, continual learning for autonomous UAVs, and large-scale data mining.
