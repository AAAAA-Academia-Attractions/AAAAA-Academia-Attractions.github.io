Zeyu Li is a Ph.D. student at Wuhan University. He received his B.Sc. in Computer Science from McGill University and his M.Sc. in Data Science from Nanyang Technological University.

His research centers on agentic AI and event-based vision, with broader interests in multimodal and embodied intelligence, vision-language-action models, and scientific automation. He develops verifiable and deployable intelligent systems for complex real-world perception and decision-making, including event-camera scene understanding, space object and debris detection, and domain-specific agent workflows.
