Zhengxi is currently an undergraduate student at MBZUAI, class of 2029. Before joining MBZUAI, he graduated from Beijing 101 High School with an International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme.

Throughout his academic journey, he has been driven by a core philosophy: correct and refine intuitions using theory and experiment. He is now particularly interested in efficient reasoning in large language models (LLM) and data synthesis for LLM agent training, with an aspiration to make meaningful research contributions to the fields.
