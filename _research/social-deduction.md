## Introduction

Social deduction environments combine hidden roles, partial observability, and free-form dialogue. They are a natural testbed for whether an agent's language is actually grounded in its trajectory, rather than scored only by win rate.

## Current direction

Current work builds replayable engines and automatic audits of utterance-level consistency, so failures such as spatial hallucination and unsupported accusation can be measured directly.
