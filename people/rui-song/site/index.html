Incoming M.Sc. student · University of Toronto

Rui Song

I work on multimodal retrieval, agent evaluation, and the data systems that make both usable in production.

I received my B.Sc. in Statistics and Computer Science, with a minor in Finance, from McGill University in 2023. In September 2026 I am starting an M.Sc. in Applied Computing at the University of Toronto.

Before the master’s I was a Data Engineering Developer II on the Data Platform and AI team at Bell Canada, where I built ingestion and transformation frameworks, self-serve pipeline tooling, and production RAG systems. Earlier I was an AI research assistant at McGill, working on recommender fairness and multi-agent reinforcement learning.

Recent papers include MINER on multimodal retrieval, QUACK (EMNLP 2026 Main) on grounding in social-deduction agents, CARE (EMNLP 2026 Findings) on privacy-compliant clinical reasoning, and a survey of discrete diffusion models.
