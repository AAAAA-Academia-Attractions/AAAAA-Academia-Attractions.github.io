Layer probing

A lightweight probe at each selected layer surfaces which dimensions already carry retrieval-relevant information.

Stage 02

Sparse fusion

Adaptive neuron-level masking keeps the useful signals and fuses them into one compact embedding. The backbone stays frozen.

Abstract

Visual document retrieval has become essential for accessing information in visually rich documents. Late-interaction retrievers achieve strong quality through fine-grained token-level matching but store hundreds of vectors per page. Dense single-vector retrievers keep storage and latency advantages, yet they lag because they compress everything into a final-layer embedding.

MINER probes and fuses internal transformer representations into one vector without modifying the backbone. Across ViDoRe V1, V2, and V3 it outperforms existing dense retrievers on the majority of benchmarks, with up to 4.5% nDCG@5 gain over its backbone, while remaining close to late-interaction quality at a fraction of the index cost.
